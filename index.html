Rail, decided

A nationwide JR Pass does not fit. Tokyo–Kyoto and Himeji–Mishima sit about 13 days apart, so one 7-day pass misses a hop and two passes overpay. Buy point-to-point. Nozomi is faster and not pass-eligible anyway.

  • NRT–Shinjuku N’EX, about ¥3,250
  • Tokyo–Kyoto Hikari, about ¥13,850 reserved
  • Kyoto–Osaka special rapid, about ¥580
  • Shin-Osaka–Hiroshima, about ¥10,300
  • Hiroshima–Himeji, mid ¥5,000s–¥6,000s
  • Himeji–Mishima, noted ¥14,070 — recheck
  • Mishima–Tokyo, about ¥4,500; Ueno–NRT Skyliner about ¥2,570

Fares are published ordinary levels, per person, and move. Reserved-seat fees sit on top of some base fares. Two people, so double the long hops. Tap a chip on the converter for the dollar figure.

History of the ground

Kabukicho was a postwar black-market and theater district that never fully became the opera house it was named for. The east side of Shinjuku Station is the 1960s consumption machine: department stores, Omoide Yokocho’s alley bars, and Golden Gai’s six narrow lanes of six-seat counters. Shinjuku Gyoen, the old imperial garden, is the quiet counterweight — and in 2026 its maples are forecast to peak late November into early December, exactly this stay.

Advisor

This hotel is a sleep base in a loud neighborhood, not a garden hotel. Use it for nights and JR departures. Do Meiji Jingu at opening, teamLab Borderless on a timed ticket, and one leaf garden (Shinjuku Gyoen or Rikugien) on a weekday morning. Golden Gai is a two-drink, cash, no-photo stop — not a bar crawl with luggage. Seats on the return and outbound cannot be chosen until 72 hours before departure; set that reminder.

Do this, not everything

  • Meiji Jingu at opening, then Harajuku before noon
  • Shinjuku Gyoen or Rikugien for koyo — Tokyo peak is late Nov to early Dec
  • teamLab Borderless, Azabudai Hills, timed entry only
  • One night in Golden Gai or Omoide Yokocho, then leave
  • Tsukiji outer market breakfast if you want the fish, not the old inner market myth

Money on the ground

Hotel is prepaid. Breakfast is optional. A Suica covers every metro day. Cash still wins in Golden Gai and some tachinomi.
